Why this matters for learning: Dense gives the agent a gradient — "I survived 50 steps last time, 60 this time, I'm improving." Sparse gives nothing until the very end — the agent has no idea if it's getting better until it either survives the full episode or fails. That's why sparse needs exploration mechanisms like RND and ICM to provide intermediate learning signal.
